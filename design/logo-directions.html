Trajan — three directions

A. The column

Trajan's Column: a single upright with the spiral relief, reduced to a vertical bar and one coil. Reads as "I" / a pillar / a receipt ribbon.

B. The market

Trajan's Market: a row of arches — the first shopping arcade. The product in one shape: stalls the state built so merchants could trade. Three arches = Set up / Get paid / Cash out.

C. The inscription

The letters at the column's base are where Roman capitals come from — the typeface "Trajan" is literally named after it. Wordmark only, in inscriptional caps, no mark. Quiet and a bit grand; risk is it reads as a movie poster.
