TRAJAN

Get paid by agents for the thing you already run.

An API, a dataset, an MCP tool. Add two lines, and every call costs what you say. Agents pay on Tempo in about a second; you watch it land.

Your passkey is your Tempo account. Face ID or Touch ID, no seed phrase, no extension. Payments go straight to it.

Step 1

SET UP

Sign up, name your seller, set a price. In your server's folder, run npx trajan-cli init --cloud and add the two lines it prints.

Step 2

GET PAID

An agent calls your tool, gets a 402 with the price, pays, and gets its answer. You see the receipt here.

Step 3

CASH OUT

Funds are already at your address — nothing is held by Trajan. Move them whenever you like.

Live on Tempo Moderato testnet during the Crypto World's Fair hackathon. Self-hosting? The open-source version runs without an account: github.com/Lideeyah/trajan.
